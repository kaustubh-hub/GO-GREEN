Other Sector Emissions

Overview

The "other" sector includes miscellaneous emissions like fluorinated gases (e.g., HFCs), accounting for 1% of global CO₂ equivalents.

Key Stats

Annual emissions from other sources: 0 billion tons CO₂e.

Environmental Impact

These gases trap heat effectively, contributing to ozone depletion and long-term climate warming.

Reduction Tips

  • Choose products without HFCs, like eco-friendly refrigerants.
  • Support policies phasing out harmful gases.
  • Opt for natural alternatives in manufacturing.

❄️ Fluorinated Gases

HFCs are used in ACs and fridges; they have a global warming potential 1,000x higher than CO₂.

🌍 Global Agreements

The Kigali Amendment aims to reduce HFC use by 80% by 2047, significantly cutting emissions.
